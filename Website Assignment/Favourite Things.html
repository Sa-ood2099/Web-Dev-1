Favourite Things

Here are some of my Favourite things.

Favourite Superhero

Spider-man


Spider-man needs no introduction, he is Marvel comics most popular superhero. beloved by many.
Everyone knows the classic tale of the kid bitten by a radioactive spider and gained special powers. And after the loss of his Uncle he would then vow to protect the innocent.
Spider-man is very special to me, as you can see from this entire site it is very themed around him, and his aethetics.
Spider-man (2002) was the first movie I remember watching, and Ultimate Spider-man was one of the first comics I remember reading. Spider-man helped shape me into the man I am now. He taught me how to purservere when things got tough and he taught me one of the most valueable lessons I've ever learned, with great power comes great responsibility. He is the character I aspire to be like in my day to day and he is in my opinion the greatest superhero ever.





My Favourite Comic Book


Batman and Robin


Batman and Robin written by Grant Morrison with art by Frank Qutely.


This is the famous Batman and Robin: Batman Reborn run, this comic takes place after Bruce Wayne died and follows the former Robin and Nightwing Dick Grayson who takes up the mantle of Batman. He has to take care of Gotham, and live up to the legacy of his mentor. In this comic he is accompanied by Bruce Wayne's son Damien Wayne. The young kid was raised by the league of shadows, and has a bit of the ruthless streak but now he wants to be someone his father could be proud of. The story follows Dick Grayson and Damian Wayne as Batman and Robin as they take on old and new threats in Gotham and continue the legacy of their father.




This one of the first Batman stories I read. Dick Grayson is my favourite DC characters and Damian is my favourite Robin. And this comic is the main reason for this. The dynamic between Dick and Damian is one of my all time favourites in comics and of all time. This comic also introduced one of my favourite villains Professor Pyg and the rivalry between Batman and Redhood in this comic is in my opinion the best dynamic between these two in comics. This comicbook is something of a comfort read for me and is one I go back to often.

My Favourite Game (Currently)


Resident Evil Reqiuem


Resident Evil Reqiuem also known as Resident Evil 9 or RE9 for short.
This game is the latest in the Resident Evil franchise, and follows two characters. Newcomer to the series Grace Ashcroft and returning character Leon S Kennedy.
The plot follows these two as they uncover the secret of Elpis and stop the schemes of Victor Giddeon and the Connections. The game has two main sections, the horror focused sections with Grace. Which is all about tense survival and resource management.
The other half follows Leon S Kennedy, a vetern Resident Evil character who returns to kick ass. His gameplay is all about aggressive combat, resource management and action set pieces.
Both styles are masterfully crafted and the two elements flow together well.
And Everything is tied together with an amazing story that though flawed is some areas, is an amazing introduction for Grace and an awesome return for Leon.
Soundtrack is also awesome.








This is game is very new but after playing it I can't stop thinking about it and talking about it. I absolutely love this game and it reignited a passion for the frachise. I just love this game and want to replay the whole thing again.


Favourite Anime


Tengan Toppa Gurren Lagan


Gurren Lagan is a story of Simon and team Dai Gurren, as they go from fighting an evil overlord and his beatmen minions to a galactic epic to save the universe. The show has amazing art, fantastic music and great voice acting. The story explores themes of brotherhood, the trap of conformity, will power and ascention.
The show feel like one big motivation speech in the best way possible, with a final message being live and strive. But also to do the right thing, even if you got to give up your own comfort for it.






This show is very special to be as it is not only my favourite anime but my favourite show of all time. This anime saved my life when I was a young teenager, at a point where I had no hope this show swooped in and showed me if I keep drilling, keep moving forward I can indeed make it. Because of that this is my favourite anime.
